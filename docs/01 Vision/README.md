# 01 Vision

The canonical, living statement of Noivos's vision, mission, and product philosophy lives in [`PROJECT_MEMORY.md`](../../PROJECT_MEMORY.md) (§1–§3), since it must never silently drift from what the founder has approved.

The Phase 1 deliverable — the [Product Requirements Document](../02%20Product%20Requirements/PRD.md) — restates and expands on vision/mission (§2–§4) in the context of the full product spec.

This folder will hold future standalone vision/strategy artifacts (e.g., pitch narrative, one-pager) as they're produced. Nothing is duplicated here to avoid two sources of truth.
